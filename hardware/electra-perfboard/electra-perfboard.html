Build order

  1. Fit the wire links on the back first, while the board is empty. Short runs can be bent component leads or solder bridges.
  2. Diodes (stripe = cathode, match the band on the drawing), then resistors.
  3. Film caps, then the transistor (flat face as drawn), then the electrolytic (+ leg on the hole marked +).
  4. Off-board wires: input jack, DC jack, ground, and the Volume pot. The pot's middle lug goes straight to the output jack tip.
  5. Power up with no guitar and measure Q1's collector (the C hole) to ground. Expect about 6–7.5 V. Near 9 V means the transistor isn't conducting (check its orientation). Near 0–1 V means it's saturated (check R1 and R2).

Diodes: the 1N5819 Schottky clips at about 0.3 V, close to the germanium diodes in the original. A pair of 1N4148 clips at about 0.6 V, so it's louder and slightly cleaner. You can try both. If you use a 2N3904, BC547 or S8050 instead of the 2SC1815, their legs are E-B-C, so cross two of them to land E, C and B on the holes marked.
