Pinouts

Wiring: pots, jacks and footswitch

Pots and switch are drawn from the back, the side you solder. Board pads are where they sit on the component side. Wires to GND can all join the ground pad G, or a bare wire run across the jacks.

  • Pot lugs, from the back (shaft pointing away, lugs at the bottom): 3 · 2 · 1 left to right. From the front they read 1 · 2 · 3. Turning clockwise moves the wiper (lug 2) toward lug 3.
  • Distortion and Filter: join lugs 2 and 3 with a short wire on each. Clockwise = more distortion / darker. Volume: its wiper (lug 2) goes straight to the footswitch; clockwise = louder.
  • 3PDT: the middle row (4, 5, 6) is the three commons. Pressing the switch flips every common between the row above and the row below. In one position the guitar goes through the board and the LED lights. In the other, the input jack goes straight to the output jack and the board input is grounded, which keeps it quiet.
  • Check your switch before wiring: with a multimeter on continuity, lug 5 should beep to lug 2 or lug 8 and never to 4 or 6. If it beeps to 4 or 6, turn the switch 90°.
  • LED: long leg (anode) to the 4.7k resistor, short leg (flat side) to lug 6. Use 2.2k for a brighter LED.
  • DC socket (2.1 mm, centre negative): the centre pin is ground, the outer sleeve is +9 V. Find which lug is which with the multimeter.

Build order

  1. Fit the wire links on the back first, while the board is empty. Use insulated wire for every dashed link: each one crosses other connections.
  2. Diodes (stripe = cathode, match the band on the drawing), then resistors.
  3. The DIP-8 socket with its notch at the end shown. Don't solder the chip itself.
  4. Film caps. For C4, twist two 220p caps end to end (series = 110p) and fit the outer legs. Then the C1815 (flat face as drawn), then the electrolytics (+ leg on the hole marked +).
  5. Off-board wires to the pots, jacks, DC socket and footswitch, following the wiring diagram.
  6. Before fitting the chip, power up and check: socket pin 8 ≈ 8.7 V, pins 3 and 5 about half that. Then fit the op-amp and check pins 1, 2, 3, 6, 7 all sit near half the supply, and Q1's E about 2.5–3 V.

Electrolytics you'll need: 2.2 µF, 2 × 4.7 µF, 2 × 1 µF, 100 µF. If your kit has no 2.2 µF, two 1 µF in parallel works; 10 µF is fine in place of 4.7 µF.
